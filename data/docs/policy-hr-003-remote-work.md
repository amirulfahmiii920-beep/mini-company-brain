# HR-POL-003 Remote Work Policy

Policy number: HR-POL-003
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 September 2026
Policy owner: Aisyah Zainal, HR Manager

## 1. Purpose

Rakan Tech is an office-first company with flexible remote work. Working in the same room
helps a small team move fast, but we also trust people to do focused work from home.

## 2. Remote days

### 2.1 How many days

Employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week. The other working days are spent at
the Kuala Lumpur office in Bangsar South.

### 2.2 Choosing your days

- Agree your remote days with your line manager.
- Every team has one shared office day each week, set by the team lead. Do not take this
  day as a remote day.
- Unused remote days do not carry over to the next week.

### 2.3 Exceptions

Working remotely for more than 2 days in a week (for example, during a family emergency)
needs written approval from HR. Line managers cannot approve extra remote days on their
own.

## 3. Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 Malaysia time, Monday to Friday. During core hours you must
be reachable on Slack and available for meetings, whether you are in the office or remote.

## 4. Equipment and security

- Use your company laptop for all work. Do not use personal devices for company or customer
  data.
- Connect through the company VPN when working outside the office.
- Do not work with customer data on public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
- See HR-POL-005 Code of Conduct, section 3, for customer data rules.

## 5. Home office allowance

Employees can claim a one-time home office allowance of up to RM300 for items such as a
chair or monitor stand. Claims follow HR-POL-004 Expenses Policy.
