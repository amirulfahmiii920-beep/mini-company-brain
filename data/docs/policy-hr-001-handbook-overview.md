# HR-POL-001 Employee Handbook Overview

Policy number: HR-POL-001
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 September 2026
Policy owner: Aisyah Zainal, HR Manager

## 1. Welcome

Welcome to Rakan Tech Sdn Bhd. We are a startup in Kuala Lumpur building RakanPay, an
invoicing app that helps Malaysian small businesses send invoices, get paid and stay
compliant with LHDN e-Invoice rules.

This handbook explains how we work together. It is split into separate policy documents so
that each one can be updated on its own. If a policy changes, HR will publish a new version
and announce it to everyone.

## 2. Our values

1. **Customers first.** Small business owners trust us with their money and their data.
   We protect both.
2. **Ship and learn.** We release small changes often and learn from real usage.
3. **Be honest.** Say what you know, say what you do not know, and raise problems early.
4. **Help each other.** Nobody at Rakan Tech works alone.

## 5. How policies are applied

- If a policy and an email or chat message disagree, the policy wins until HR publishes a
  new version.
- Managers may not change a policy for their team. Exceptions must be approved in writing
  by HR.
- Questions about a policy should go to HR, not be guessed.

## 6. Confidential HR records

Salaries, performance reviews and disciplinary records are confidential. They are kept by
HR and are only visible to the HR team and the people directly involved.
