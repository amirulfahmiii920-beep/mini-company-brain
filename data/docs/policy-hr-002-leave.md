# HR-POL-002 Leave Policy

Policy number: HR-POL-002
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 September 2026
Policy owner: Aisyah Zainal, HR Manager

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how much leave employees of Rakan Tech Sdn Bhd get and how to apply
for it. Our entitlements are equal to or better than the minimum in the Malaysian
Employment Act 1955.

## 2. Annual leave

### 2.1 Entitlement

Annual leave is based on length of service:

| Length of service | Annual leave per calendar year |
| --- | --- |
| Less than 2 years (including new hires) | 14 days |
| 2 years to less than 5 years | 16 days |
| 5 years or more | 18 days |

New hires are entitled to 14 days of annual leave per calendar year from their start date.

### 2.2 Applying for annual leave

- Apply in the HR system at least 3 working days in advance for leave of 1 or 2 days.
- Apply at least 2 weeks in advance for leave of 3 days or more.
- Your line manager approves the request. HR keeps the record.

### 2.3 Carry forward

Up to 5 unused days can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Carried-forward days
must be used by 31 March of that year or they are lost.

## 3. Sick leave

### 3.1 Outpatient sick leave

Paid outpatient sick leave follows the tiers in the Employment Act 1955 and is based on
length of service:

| Length of service | Outpatient sick leave per calendar year |
| --- | --- |
| Less than 2 years (including new hires) | 14 days |
| 2 years to less than 5 years | 18 days |
| 5 years or more | 22 days |

A medical certificate (MC) from a registered doctor is required for every day of sick
leave.

### 3.2 Hospitalisation leave

Employees get up to 60 days of paid hospitalisation leave per calendar year, including the
outpatient sick leave days already taken.

### 3.3 Reporting

Tell your line manager before 10:00 on the day you are sick, and upload the MC to the HR
system within 2 working days.

## 4. Parental leave

### 4.1 Maternity leave

Eligible employees get 98 consecutive days of paid maternity leave.

### 4.2 Paternity leave

Eligible employees get 7 consecutive days of paid paternity leave per birth.

## 5. Public holidays

Employees get all federal public holidays observed in the Federal Territory of Kuala
Lumpur. HR publishes the list for the next year every December. If you are asked to work
on a public holiday, you get a replacement day off.

## 6. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave may be approved by HR in special cases, for up to 30 days per calendar year.
