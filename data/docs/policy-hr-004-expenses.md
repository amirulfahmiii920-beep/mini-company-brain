# HR-POL-004 Expenses Policy

Policy number: HR-POL-004
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 September 2026
Policy owner: Kumar Selvam, Finance Manager (with Aisyah Zainal, HR Manager)

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which work expenses Rakan Tech pays back, the limits, and how to
claim.

## 2. What you can claim

| Expense | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Meals during work travel or client visits | RM50 per day |
| Mileage for work travel in your own car | RM0.60 per km |
| Parking and tolls for work travel | Actual cost, with receipt |
| Grab or taxi for work travel | Actual cost, with receipt |
| Client entertainment | RM200 per event, approved in advance |
| Home office allowance (one-time) | RM300, see HR-POL-003 section 5 |
| Training and courses | Approved in advance by your line manager |

Travel between your home and the office is not claimable.

## 3. How to claim

### 3.1 Deadline

Submit your claim in the finance system within 30 days of the date of the expense. Claims
submitted after 30 days will be rejected unless Kumar Selvam approves a late claim in
writing.

### 3.2 Receipts

Attach a clear photo or PDF of the receipt for every item. Claims without receipts are not
paid.

### 3.3 Approval

- All claims are approved by Kumar Selvam, Finance Manager.
- Claims above RM1,000 also need approval from the CEO, Hafiz Rahman.

### 3.4 Payment

Approved claims are paid together with the next monthly salary.

## 4. What we do not pay for

- Alcohol
- Traffic fines and parking summonses
- Personal items, even when bought during a work trip

## 5. Questions

Contact Kumar Selvam, Finance Manager.
