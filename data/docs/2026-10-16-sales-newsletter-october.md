# RakanPay Customer Newsletter: October 2026

From: Farah Aziz, Head of Sales and Marketing
To: RakanPay customers
Date: 16 October 2026

Hello from the RakanPay team!

## RakanPay v2 arrives on 1 December 2026

We are excited to share that **RakanPay v2 launches on 1 December 2026**. Here is what is
coming:

- **Get paid faster.** Every invoice gets a "Pay now" button, so your clients can pay by
  card in seconds. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.
- **Recurring invoices.** Send the same invoice every week, month or year, automatically.
- **RakanPay mobile app.** Create invoices and check who has paid, right from your phone.

Want to try it first? Reply to this email to join our early access list. Early access
customers get 3 months of v2 features at no extra cost.

## e-Invoice is on the way

We know many of you are preparing for LHDN e-Invoicing. Our team is building one-click
submission to MyInvois directly from RakanPay. We will share more details early next year.

## Tip of the month

Add your bank details to your invoice template so clients always know how to pay you.

Thank you for growing with RakanPay.
