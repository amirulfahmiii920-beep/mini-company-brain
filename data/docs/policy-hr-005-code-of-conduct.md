# HR-POL-005 Code of Conduct

Policy number: HR-POL-005
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 September 2026
Policy owner: Aisyah Zainal, HR Manager

## 1. Purpose

This code describes how we expect everyone at Rakan Tech Sdn Bhd to behave, how we handle
customer data, and what happens when the code is broken.

## 2. Behaviour at work

- Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect.
- Harassment, bullying and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.
- Declare any conflict of interest (for example, a side business that sells to our
  customers) to HR.
- Do not accept gifts worth more than RM100 from customers or vendors.

## 3. Customer data handling

RakanPay holds invoices, payment records and business details of our customers. This data
is protected under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

### 3.1 Rules

1. Access customer data only when you need it for your work.
2. Never copy customer data, including test datasets made from customer data, to personal
   devices, personal email or personal cloud storage (such as a personal Google Drive or
   Dropbox).
3. Use only the anonymised test datasets in the company test environment for development
   and QA.
4. Report any data leak, or suspected leak, to the CTO, Tan Mei Ling, within 24 hours.

### 3.2 Breaches

Breaking section 3.1 is treated as misconduct and follows the disciplinary process in
section 4.

## 4. Disciplinary process

### 4.1 Steps

For most breaches of this code, the steps are:

1. **Verbal warning**, recorded by HR.
2. **Written warning**, kept on the employee's HR file for 12 months.
3. **Final written warning**, kept on the employee's HR file for 12 months.
4. **Dismissal**, only after a domestic inquiry.

### 4.2 Serious misconduct

For serious misconduct (for example theft, fraud or deliberate leaking of customer data),
the company may skip steps and go straight to a domestic inquiry.

### 4.3 Fairness

The employee is told what they are accused of, can respond in writing, and can bring a
colleague to any disciplinary meeting. All disciplinary records are confidential and held
by HR.
