# Disciplinary Record: Written Warning, Jason Lim

CONFIDENTIAL. HR record. Visible to HR, the employee and the manager only.

Employee: Jason Lim, QA Engineer
Manager: Tan Mei Ling, CTO
HR: Aisyah Zainal, HR Manager
Date of meeting: 6 October 2026
Policy: HR-POL-005 Code of Conduct, section 3.1 rule 2 (customer data handling)

## 1. What happened

- On 27 September 2026, Jason copied a test dataset built from real customer invoices (not
  the anonymised test dataset) from the staging environment to a personal Google Drive
  account, to continue testing over the weekend.
- On 1 October 2026, a routine access review by Mei Ling found the download. Jason
  confirmed it the same day and deleted the files from the personal Google Drive in front
  of Mei Ling.
- The review found no evidence that the files were shared with anyone else.
- Mei Ling recorded the incident as a data handling breach and reviewed it against the
  company's PDPA obligations.

## 2. Previous record

- Verbal warning on 4 August 2026 for posting staging credentials in a public Slack
  channel.

## 3. Outcome

- Because there is a previous verbal warning, this is a **written warning**, step 2 of the
  disciplinary process in HR-POL-005 section 4.1.
- The warning stays on Jason's HR file for 12 months, until 6 October 2027.
- A further breach of HR-POL-005 within 12 months may lead to a final written warning.

## 4. Actions agreed

1. Jason completes the internal data protection training by 20 October 2026.
2. Jason uses only the anonymised test dataset for all QA work.
3. Mei Ling restricts staging data downloads to the anonymised dataset by 13 October 2026.

## 5. Employee response

"I understand why this was wrong. I wanted to finish testing before the deadline and did
not think about where the data was going. It will not happen again." (Jason Lim)
