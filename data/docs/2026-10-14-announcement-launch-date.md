# RakanPay v2 Launch Moves to 15 December 2026

From: Hafiz Rahman, CEO
To: All staff
Date: 14 October 2026

Hi everyone,

At the leadership meeting on 12 October 2026 we agreed to move the public launch of
RakanPay v2 from **1 December 2026** to **15 December 2026**.

## Why

1. **Stripe account review.** Stripe's verification and payout review for our merchant
   account is taking longer than planned. We do not want to launch before payouts are
   fully approved.
2. **QA time.** Jason needs more time for full regression and load testing of payments and
   recurring invoices.
3. **Mobile app.** Wei Jie joins on 13 October. Two extra weeks give the mobile app a
   realistic schedule.

## New timeline

| Milestone | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Feature complete | 24 November 2026 |
| Code freeze | 1 December 2026 |
| Final QA and fixes | 1 to 12 December 2026 |
| **Public launch** | **15 December 2026** |

## What this means for you

- **Sales and marketing:** please update all customer communication to say 15 December.
  Do not mention 1 December to customers any more.
- **Engineering:** see the updated plan from Priya in #rakanpay-v2.
- **LHDN e-Invoice integration:** not affected. The target for full sandbox validation is
  still 30 November 2026.

Thank you for the hard work. A slightly later launch that works is better than an early
one that does not.

Hafiz
